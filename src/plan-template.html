Why he should say yes

  1. His business card already promises a website that doesn't exist.The card photographed on his Yelp listing prints MojaveMedical.org. That domain is unregistered as of today. Register it before someone else does.
  2. He moved, and the internet has not noticed.Mojave Medical is now at 16041 Kamana Rd, confirmed by his own Google Business Profile and by a photograph of his new building signage. Every other source still sends patients to 15982 Tuscola Rd, Suite B, because his federal provider record has not been updated since July 2025. Yelp, Healthgrades, Doximity, Providence, Practo and Vitals all mirror the stale address. Patients are being directed to the wrong building today.
  3. Third-party directories are his homepage right now, and they're wrong.Practo calls him "Mr. Kevin Ganesh" with "Free Consultation" and 9 AM to 9 PM seven days a week. Vitals lists his primary office in Richmond, Virginia. Doximity shows a phantom Stanford gastroenterology residency. Healthgrades and Yelp disagree on hours. A site of record fixes the source of truth.
  4. He is the only board-certified ID physician in Apple Valley without a website.Dr. Vincent Covelli at Providence has MyChart, online scheduling and video visits. Dr. Mallad is closed to new patients. Dr. Bhagat in Victorville has no site. The new-patient market is open.
  5. His one negative review is about the front desk and payment, not medicine.Online booking, clear payment information and a "we confirm by text" promise fix that structurally, while the site presents the three five-star reviews he already has.
  6. Four other "Mojave Medical" businesses and Ganesh Medical Center at the same address confuse search engines.Schema markup, "Apple Valley" in every title, "Suite B" everywhere and a footer disambiguation line separate his brand.
  7. Unclaimed local-search niches.Valley Fever, HIV and PrEP, travel medicine, post-discharge IV-antibiotic follow-up, and telehealth for Barstow and Lucerne Valley patients who currently drive to Loma Linda.

Behaviour

Sticky header with the amber Book button; a fixed Call/Book bar on phones; full English/Spanish toggle on every page including the booking flow; light and dark themes; page transitions and a slow 60-second rotation on the mark, all disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. Backend, booking and hosting

The appointment flow is not a mockup. It is a working Node service: the form posts to an API, requests are stored in SQLite on a persistent volume, and staff see them on a dashboard at /admin where they can filter, mark contacted, add notes and export CSV. Everything is built, tested end to end, and containerised for Coolify.

Question you askedAnswer
Where do bookings go?A POST /api/appointments endpoint writes to SQLite at /app/data/mojave.db on a Docker volume, so redeploys never lose data.
Where do you see the leads?/admin, behind HTTP Basic auth. Status tiles, search, per-request detail, staff notes, one-click status changes, CSV export, and an append-only audit log of every view and change.
Where do form emails land?Whatever you set MAIL_TO to, e.g. the front desk mailbox. If SMTP is left unset, email is simply off and requests still land in the dashboard. Nothing is lost.
What SMTP does it use?Any provider, via SMTP_HOST / SMTP_USER / SMTP_PASS. For a US medical practice the provider must sign a BAA. See the table below.
Can it run on Coolify with Docker?Yes. Dockerfile plus compose file are in the repo, and DEPLOY.md is a step-by-step Coolify guide.

The email design decision

By default the staff notification email contains only a reference number and the visit type. The patient's name, date of birth and reason for visit stay behind the dashboard login. That is deliberate: US federal regulators treat information submitted on a medical practice's appointment form as protected health information from the moment it is submitted, even for someone who has never been a patient. Keeping it out of mail servers shrinks the blast radius and the number of vendors who need a signed agreement. A single environment variable turns full-detail emails on once the agreements are in place.

Coolify deployment

Use the Dockerfile build pack, not Docker Compose. Compose applications in Coolify give up rolling updates and force storage, environment and health configuration into the compose file instead of the UI. The specifics that actually bite:

  • Bind to 0.0.0.0, never 127.0.0.1. This is the most common cause of a 502 on Coolify.
  • Set Ports Exposes to 3000 and leave Ports Mappings empty. Traefik routes from the label Coolify generates, not from your PORT variable.
  • Named volume at /app/data, never a bind mount, or the non-root container hits a permission error on every redeploy.
  • Turn on "Consistent Container Name" to disable rolling updates, so two containers never hold the same SQLite file open. Ten seconds of downtime per deploy beats a corrupted database.
  • Health check on /healthz with a 20 second start period. A zero second start period marks a booting Node process unhealthy and rolls the deploy back.
  • Back up with Coolify's native scheduled volume backup to S3, with "Stop During Backup" on. Test a restore quarterly.

Analytics

Use privacy-first analytics such as Plausible or Fathom, and keep any analytics off the booking form, its confirmation screen and the dashboard. A form submission is affirmative submission of identified health information. Meta Pixel is hard to justify on a medical site at all, and state wiretapping litigation is now a bigger practical exposure than federal enforcement.
